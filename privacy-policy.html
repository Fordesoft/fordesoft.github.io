1. Introduction

Fordesoft, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Eternal Exodus website (www.eternalexodus.com) (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit our Site. It applies to all visitors worldwide, including residents of the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom (UK), California, and other U.S. states with comprehensive privacy laws.

By using the Site, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. Where required by law, we will obtain your consent before collecting or processing your personal data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect limited information through cookies and similar tracking technologies:

2.1 Information Collected Automatically

  • Analytics data (via Google Analytics): pages visited, time on page, referring URL, browser type, device type, screen resolution, approximate geographic location (city/country level), and session behavior.
  • Advertising/marketing data (via Vibe pixel and Roku pixel): page view events, browser and device identifiers, and data used to measure ad campaign effectiveness.

2.2 Information You Provide Voluntarily

  • Email address: if you subscribe to our mailing list via the Mailchimp signup form on the Site.

2.3 Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect names, physical addresses, payment information, or any sensitive personal data through this Site.

3. Legal Bases for Processing (EEA/UK)

If you are in the EEA or UK, we process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR): For setting non-essential cookies (analytics and marketing). You may grant or withdraw consent at any time via our cookie consent banner.
  • Legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): For essential Site functionality (e.g., remembering your consent preference).

4. How We Use Information

  • To understand how visitors interact with the Site and improve its content and performance.
  • To measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns.
  • To send release notifications to subscribers who have opted in via the mailing list.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

7. Your Privacy Rights

7.1 EEA / UK Residents (GDPR / UK GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erase your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restrict or object to processing.
  • Data portability.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing).
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Fordesoft, LLC does not currently meet the thresholds requiring appointment of a Data Protection Officer (DPO) under GDPR. For all privacy inquiries, please contact us using the details in Section 13 below.

7.2 California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act and the California Privacy Rights Act, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, disclose, and sell/share.
  • Delete your personal information.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Limit the use of sensitive personal information (we do not collect sensitive PI).
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your rights.

The Vibe and Roku advertising pixels may constitute a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under CCPA/CPRA. You may opt out at any time by clicking "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" in the footer of our Site, or by using our cookie consent banner to decline marketing cookies.

7.3 Other U.S. State Privacy Laws

If you are a resident of Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana, or another state with comprehensive privacy legislation, you may have similar rights to access, delete, correct, and opt out of targeted advertising and sales of personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address in Section 13 or use the cookie consent controls on our Site.

7.4 Global Privacy Control (GPC)

We honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal. If your browser sends a GPC signal, we will automatically treat it as a request to opt out of the sale/sharing of your personal information and will not load marketing cookies.

8. Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

We may "sell" or "share" personal information (as defined under applicable U.S. state laws) through the use of advertising pixels (Vibe and Roku) that enable third parties to track your browsing activity for targeted advertising purposes.

You can opt out of this at any time by:

  • Clicking "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" in the Site footer.
  • Declining marketing cookies in our consent banner.
  • Enabling the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal in your browser.

9. Data Retention

  • Analytics data (Google Analytics): retained for up to 26 months, then automatically deleted.
  • Advertising pixel data (Vibe, Roku): retained according to each provider's data retention policy.
  • Consent records (Cookiebot): retained for 12 months from the date of consent.
  • Email addresses (Mailchimp): retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion.

This Site is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact us immediately at the address below so we can delete it.
